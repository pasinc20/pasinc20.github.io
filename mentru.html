Say what you want to do.
Then actually do it.

Mentru turns a conversation about your goal into milestones and daily tasks, then checks back in to keep you moving — for people who want to stop procrastinating and follow through.

Download on the App Store Support

Ask by voice

Live voice chat for natural conversation. Tap Mentru in the app, or from a Home Screen or Lock Screen widget, ask what is left today, and hear the answer aloud.

Goals into daily actions

Create multiple plans, break each into milestones and practical daily tasks, and mark work complete as you go.

Follow-ups that land

Choose up to seven daily check-ins in your own time zone. Mentru reminds you when work is still open and recognises the progress you have made.

Accountability calls

Optional calls arrive through the iPhone call interface and carry on the conversation about your plan.

Habits and streaks

Review completed and incomplete days in the Progress calendar. Open any date to see what was recorded and follow your streak.

Come back to it

Finish a plan to archive it, or return to an earlier plan whenever you need it again.

How it works

1

Say the goal

Talk or type about what you want to achieve. No forms to fill in first.

2

Get a plan

Mentru turns the conversation into milestones and daily tasks you can actually do.

3

Be checked on

Daily check-ins at times you choose, by notification or by call, until it is done.
