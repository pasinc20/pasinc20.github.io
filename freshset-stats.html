Freshset subscriptions

Connecting…

Live counts from Apple, delivered the moment a subscription event happens rather than waiting for the next day's sales report. Aggregate totals only — no customer information passes through here, and Freshset has no accounts to identify anyone by.

Free trials started
–
First-time subscribers who began the three-day trial.
Payments taken
–
Every successful charge: trials converting, weekly renewals, and anyone who subscribed without a trial.
Auto-renew turned off
–
Cancellations, counted the moment they happen — usually well before the subscription actually lapses.

How this works. Apple sends an App Store Server Notification to the Freshset relay within seconds of each event. Every notification is verified against Apple's own certificate chain before it is counted, so the numbers cannot be inflated by anyone who finds the endpoint. Sandbox and TestFlight purchases are counted separately and never appear here. Counters started at zero when this page went up, so they are a running total from that point, not lifetime figures.
